# Services and pricing

Demo business: a Charlotte, NC home-services company (HVAC, plumbing, water heaters).
These are sample prices for the hackathon demo, not real quotes.

## Diagnostic visit
- Standard diagnostic visit: $89. Waived if you approve the repair on the same visit.
- After-hours or weekend emergency visit: $149.

## Common jobs (starting prices)
- AC tune-up: $129
- AC repair (most common fixes such as capacitor or contactor): from $185
- Furnace repair: from $175
- Drain clearing (single line): from $149
- Leak repair under a sink or behind a toilet: from $165
- Water heater flush: $119
- Water heater replacement (40–50 gallon tank, installed): from $1,650

## How quotes work
- The technician gives a fixed written price before starting any work.
- No charge for the estimate on replacements (new AC system or water heater).
- We accept card, cash, and financing on jobs over $1,000.
