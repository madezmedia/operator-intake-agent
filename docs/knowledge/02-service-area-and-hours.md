# Service area and hours

## Where we work
- Charlotte and Mecklenburg County, including ZIP codes 28202–28277.
- Also Matthews, Mint Hill, Pineville, Huntersville and Cornelius.
- Outside these areas: we can usually still come out, with a $40 travel fee.

## Hours
- Office and booking: Monday–Friday 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM (Eastern).
- Appointment windows: 9:00, 11:00, 1:00 and 3:00 on weekdays.
- Emergency service (no heat, no cooling in extreme weather, active leaks): 24/7.
- The online assistant takes requests any time, day or night, and books the next open window.

## What to expect
- You get a text confirmation when you book and a reminder the evening before.
- The technician texts when they are on the way, usually 30–60 minutes ahead.
