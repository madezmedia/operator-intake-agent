# Warranty and policies

## Warranty
- Labor on repairs: 1 year.
- Parts: manufacturer warranty, usually 1–10 years depending on the part.
- New AC systems and water heaters: 10-year parts and 2-year labor when registered.

## Rescheduling and cancelling
- Reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 2 hours before your window.
- Missed appointment with no notice: we will reach out to rebook, no fee.

## Licensing
- Licensed and insured in North Carolina for HVAC and plumbing.
- All technicians are background-checked.

## Who we can help
- Homeowners and renters. Renters: we need the landlord's OK for replacements, not for repairs.
- We do residential work only, no commercial buildings.
